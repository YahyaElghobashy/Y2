# Food decode — what everything is, what to never miss

Open flag: **pork + alcohol in or out?** (open question #1). Items marked 🐷 are pork-based or pork-heavy venues; ⚠️ = commonly contains pork unless you ask. Everything unmarked at a mamak/Malay/Indian-Muslim place is halal by default.

## Malaysia

| Thing | What it is | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Nasi lemak** | Coconut rice, sambal chili, fried anchovies, peanuts, egg; add fried chicken. National dish. | **Never-miss.** Village Park (Damansara) = the benchmark; Kampung Baru stalls = the soul version. |
| **Roti canai** | Flaky griddled flatbread dunked in dal/curry. RM2. 24h at any mamak. | **Never-miss.** The 2am mamak run is the most KL thing that exists. |
| **Teh tarik** | "Pulled" milk tea, poured from height till frothy. | Auto-order with every roti. |
| **Satay** | Charcoal skewers + peanut sauce (chicken/beef). | **Never-miss.** Kampung Baru or Jalan Alor. |
| **Nasi kandar** | Rice drowned in 3–4 mixed curries, Indian-Muslim style. Halal. | Must if you're a sauce person. Pelita (24h, nr KLCC) is the reliable one. |
| **Banana leaf rice** | South Indian: rice on a leaf, veg sides, curries, unlimited refills, eat by hand. | **Never-miss.** Brickfields, Thursday lunch slot. |
| **Assam laksa** | Sour-spicy mackerel broth, thick rice noodles, mint, pineapple. Funky. | Adventurous must. Madras Lane stalls (lunch only, closed Mon). |
| **Curry mee / curry laksa** | Coconut-curry noodle soup. | Great. ⚠️ KL versions often add pork blood cubes — Madras Lane one does; ask "no pork" or skip. |
| **Yong tau foo** | Tofu/veg stuffed with fish paste, pick-your-own bowl. Mild. | Safe, good, not life-changing. |
| **Char kway teow** | Smoky wok-fried flat noodles, prawn. | ⚠️ Usually lard + sometimes Chinese sausage. Superb if pork ok. |
| **Bak kut teh** 🐷 | Pork rib herbal soup, invented in Klang. | If pork out → dead, skip Klang entirely (45 min away, no slot anyway). If pork in → it's still 45 min away; a KL branch is the compromise, Klang purists will forgive you. |
| **Hutong @ Lot 10** 🐷 | Heritage hawker basement, 40-year lineages. | Honest flag: the famous stalls are heavily **non-halal** (Kim Lian Kee hokkien mee = lard). Pork in → best single intro to Malaysian food, true. Pork out → skip; Jalan Alor + mamaks serve you better. |
| **Jalan Alor** | Night food street. Mixed halal/non stalls. | **Non-negotiable** (your word, correct). Wong Ah Wah grilled chicken wings = the anchor order. |
| **Durian (Musang King)** | Creamy, bitter-sweet, smells like a crime. Stalls near Jalan Alor. | One seed minimum. Committing = Musang King. No durian into hotels/trains — genuinely banned. |
| Hidden: **Lucky Garden, Bangsar** | Morning hawker stalls, zero tourists. | Only lives if Sat Option B. |
| Hidden: **Cyberjaya student mamaks** | Cheap, 24h, real. | Friday night default. |

## Vietnam (Hanoi + Sapa)

| Thing | What it is | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Phở bò** | Beef noodle soup, breakfast food. | **Never-miss.** Phở Gia Truyền, 49 Bát Đàn — the queue one. Cash, fast line, brisk service = the experience. |
| **Bún chả** 🐷 | Char-grilled pork patties in fish-sauce broth + noodles + herb pile. The Obama dish. | Pork in → **#1 Hanoi lunch**, no debate. Đắc Kim (1 Hàng Mành) famous; the Hàng Quạt alley one is the local pick. Pork out → substitute bún bò Nam Bộ (dry beef noodles) — excellent. |
| **Bánh mì** | Baguette, pâté⚠️/meats/herbs/chili. | Bánh Mì 25 = famous and actually good; ask fillings (pâté/chả often pork; chicken/beef/egg versions everywhere). |
| **Chả cá** | Turmeric fish + dill, sizzling tableside brazier. Hanoi-only dish. | Farewell-dinner grade. Chả Cá Thăng Long. |
| **Bánh cuốn** | Steamed rice rolls ⚠️ (usually pork-mushroom filling; shrimp/egg variants exist). | Breakfast gem. |
| **Bún riêu** | Crab-tomato noodle soup, funky-bright. | Adventurous must. |
| **Xôi** | Savory sticky rice + toppings. | Xôi Yến or street carts. Solid breakfast #2. |
| **Phở cuốn, Ngũ Xã** | Fresh rice-sheet rolls with beef+herbs; island neighborhood locals-only. | Hidden-gem lunch, Mon 5. |
| **Egg coffee** | Whipped yolk + condensed milk over robusta. Dessert pretending to be coffee. | **Never-miss.** Cafe Giang, 39 Nguyễn Hữu Huân — the inventor's family. Order the hot one. |
| **Bia hơi** | Fresh daily-brewed draft, ~15k VND. | Tạ Hiện corner stools = the scene. If no alcohol: same stools, sugarcane juice (nước mía), zero awkwardness. |
| **Sapa: salmon/sturgeon hotpot** | Local cold-water farms. Sounds like a trap, isn't. | Cold-evening dinner, share-size. |
| **Sapa: market skewers + black chicken** | Grill-your-pick at the market. ⚠️ mixed meats incl pork. | Fun, cheap, point at what you want. |
| **Sapa: thắng cố** | Traditional horse-offal stew. | Skip. Cultural honesty > your stomach's ruin. |

## Rules that keep it good
- Queue of locals > any list, including mine. One-dish stalls > 40-item menus.
- Busy stall = high turnover = safe. Empty famous place at odd hours = worst of both.
- In Hanoi eat at Hanoi hours: phở 07:00–09:00, bún chả 11:30–13:00 — kitchens genuinely run out.
- Herbs on the side: fine at busy places; skip raw herbs only if your stomach's already complaining.
