# Bookings + admin — checklist (3 Sep 2026)

## 1. Vietnam e-visa — DO TODAY
- **evisa.gov.vn only.** The .com/.org clones are middlemen charging 3×. Official = $25, single entry (KL→HAN→KL needs no re-entry).
- Entry date: **26 Sep** (buffer before actual 28 Sep entry). 30-day single covers the whole stay with margin — matches your "wider than travel dates" note.
- Needs: passport scan (6+ months validity), face photo, hotel address (put Hanoi hotel), entry gate = Noi Bai Int'l Airport.
- Claimed 3–5 working days; often slower. Today is Thu 3 Sep — applying now lands it ~mid next week with 2 weeks spare.
- **Print ×2.** One in bag, one in luggage. Name/passport-number typos = refused boarding; triple-check before submit.

## 2. Malaysia entry — MDAC ×2
- Malaysia Digital Arrival Card: required, free, official immigration portal (imigresen.gov.my → MDAC). Submit **≤3 days before each entry**:
  - [ ] ~21–23 Sep for the 24 Sep arrival
  - [ ] ~3–5 Oct (from Vietnam) for the 6 Oct re-entry
- Egyptians: typically visa-free 90 days — confirm current rule once when filing MDAC.

## 3. Flights to buy (this week — 3 weeks out is the right window)

## 4. Night train Hanoi → Lào Cai, Wed 30 Sep
- Private-operator soft sleeper on the SP service: **Chapa Express / King Express / Sapaly / Vietage-style cabins**, ~$40–55/berth. Book via operator site or 12go.
- **Lower berth** (back). 4-berth cabin standard; some sell 2-berth upgrades ~$70–90 — mid-range worthy if sharing bothers you.
- Dep ~21:30–22:00 Hanoi (Trần Quý Cáp gate), arr Lào Cai ~05:30. Van to Sapa often bundled — take the bundle.
- Book by ~mid-Sep; Golden Week pressure starts around your dates.

## 5. Sapa → Hanoi, Sun 4 Oct (recommended: van)
- Limousine van (Sapa Express, Green Bus, Eco Sapa): ~13:30 dep, ~19:30 arr, ~350–450k VND, hotel pickup. Book 1–2 days ahead in Sapa, or online now for peace of mind.

## 6. Hotels
- [ ] **Hanoi** 28–29 Sep + 4–5 Oct (4 nights, same property both stints = they'll hold luggage patterns, know you). North Old Quarter near Hàng Bồ/Hàng Bạc = walk everywhere. Mid-range: Hanoi La Siesta family ($60–80), solid hostel privates: Little Charm, Nexy ($25–40). Ask for **quiet-side room** — Old Quarter is loud; pack earplugs regardless. Confirm early-morning luggage hold + shower-after-checkout for the train day.
- [ ] **Sapa** 1–3 Oct (3 nights). Town-edge with valley view beats town-center (construction noise is real): e.g. Sapa Relax Hotel & Spa (~$45), Pao's (~$80 splurge). Valley alternative: Tả Van eco-lodge — better views, 20-min taxi tax each way to town/Fansipan. **Book now — Golden Week Oct 1–7.**
- [ ] **KLIA, 6 Oct**: Sama-Sama Hotel (T1, 24/7 5-min shuttle, ~RM400) or CapsuleTransit @ T2 (~RM150–250). MH lands T1, QR departs T1 → Sama-Sama is the friction-free pick.
- Verify current prices/reviews at booking time — my figures age.

## 7. Luggage / Nader — the bug and the fix
- Bug: bag stored at Nader's, but you never pass his place again — you land 16:40 on 6 Oct and sleep at the airport.
- Fix: **Nader meets you twice** — Sun 27 Sep evening at your KL hotel (handoff), Tue 6 Oct evening at Mitsui/airport hotel (return). Confirm he's genuinely up for leg 2.
- Fallback: KLIA T1 left-luggage, ~RM40–55/bag/day → ~RM350–450 for 8 nights. Nader is cheaper; buy him a serious gift (Sapa or Mitsui solves this).
- Packing shape: big bag lives in KL. Vietnam = cabin-size + daypack. Sapa layers (15–25°C, rain shell), one warm layer for Fansipan summit, waterproof pouch from SplashMania reused for boat trips.

## 8. Money / SIM / apps
- **Malaysia:** cards + contactless nearly everywhere; RM200–300 cash for hawkers/Grab top-ups. Grab app works with foreign card.
- **Vietnam:** cash country. ATM at arrivals (VPBank/TPBank = low/no fee, 3–5M VND per pull). ~25k VND = $1. Grab (bikes = fastest in Old Quarter — helmet provided; car if back says no).
- eSIM: Airalo/Holafly regional, or local counters (Hotlink at KLIA, Viettel at Nội Bài — Viettel ~120–200k for tourist data pack).
- Apps: Grab, Google Maps offline (KL + Hanoi + Sapa), Google Translate offline Vietnamese, airline apps.
