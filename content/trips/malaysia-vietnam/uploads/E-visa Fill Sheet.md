## After submitting
- **Save the new registration code immediately.** Check status at https://evisa.gov.vn/e-visa/search (needs code + registration email + DOB + captcha).
- **Check the status page 2–3× daily, for BOTH applications.** Amendment requests are frequently **not** emailed — the only reliable signal is the status page. If either moves to **"Amendment required"**, that unlocks free editing: *"Please enter registration code, registration email and date of birth in E-visa search menu to update application. No extra fees required."*
- **Processing: 3 working days normally — but 3 to 15 working days if the Police Department triggers verification.** Weekends and public holidays don't count. That 15-day tail is the entire reason for reapplying immediately rather than waiting.
- **When the correct PDF arrives:** print ×2 + phone copy, **and also print the status-page record** — since the airport check is a human comparing your printout to your passport, the status page is the only official document showing exactly how the Department holds your name.
- **Then** book AK512 + Batik OD572. Backstop: if nothing has landed by **Thu 17 Sep**, book the flights anyway.
- ⚠️ **If application #1 issues with the typo, do NOT travel on it.** An issued e-visa cannot be amended. Wait for #2.

## Only these domains are real
`evisa.gov.vn` · `thithucdientu.gov.vn` · `xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn` · `immigration.gov.vn`
Everything else is an agency — including `vietnamimmigration.com` (calls itself "official") and `evisavietnam.gov1.vn` (**gov1.vn is not a government domain**). No official expedited service exists; anyone selling a "2-hour e-visa" is reselling this same $25 form.
