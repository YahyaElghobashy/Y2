**You have picked the wet end.** October's rain declines *within* the month; daily rainfall peaks around **4 October (>33 mm)** and falls to 2–3 mm/day by month's end. Expect rain, drizzle or heavy mist on **2–3 of the 4 days**, and mountain views that come and go.

**The physics that decides everything.** Mean October conditions (15.7 °C @ 90.8% RH) give a **dew point of 14.20 °C**. The mean daily minimum, 13.7 °C, is **0.5 °C BELOW that** — so on an average October night the air reaches **saturation**. The famous Sapa mist is the climate condensing. In an unheated room every surface — tile floor, uninsulated concrete wall, bedding, towels, the clothes in your bag — sits at or below the condensation point all night. Mould germinates above ~70–80% sustained surface RH; ambient here is 90.8%. This is why "damp" is the single most consistent complaint across ~18 Sapa properties from hostels to 4-star, at every price tier — a climate problem, not bad housekeeping.

**Verdicts:**
- **Cooling: not needed, and actively harmful.** Cool 15.7 → 15.0 °C and RH rises to 95%; cool to 14.0 °C and you hit **100% — saturation**. You would be manufacturing condensation. Zero complaints about hot rooms in Sep/Oct/Nov exist anywhere in the review corpus.
- **Heating: the correct dehumidifier.** Warming air without adding moisture cuts *relative* humidity: 18 °C → 78.5% · 20 °C → 69.3% · **21 °C → 65.1%** · 23 °C → 57.7%. A **~5 °C lift cuts RH by ~26 points** and, crucially, raises every surface above the 14.2 °C dew point — which is what stops damp bedding and mouldy walls. Run it an hour or two before bed. Not for warmth (14 °C under a duvet is fine) — **for moisture control.**
- **Electric blankets** warm the bed but do nothing for damp towels, musty air or mould.
- **Screen 1–3★ reviews for "damp / musty / mould / smell"** — that predicts the experience better than the star rating. Prefer newer builds and upper floors; the black-mould reports skew hard to older concrete.
- **PACKING CONSEQUENCE: synthetic or merino, NOT cotton.** Cotton will not dry at 91% RH and Sapa laundry is limited. Bring spares.
