## THU 24 SEP — land + KL Sentral spine (only full-ish KL day)

Rule: **no nap**. No room until 14:00 anyway. Daylight + walking resets the clock; sleep 22:00.
Jet lag math: body is 5h behind (Cairo). Afternoon slump 15:00–17:00 → that's why the museum (aircon, benches) sits there.

- 07:55 land T1. Immigration + bags ~60–75 min. Get MYR at ATM, eSIM if not done.
- 09:15 KLIA Ekspres → 09:45 KL Sentral. Bags → left-luggage counter (Level 1, ~RM10–30/bag/day).
  - Alt: KLIA Transit to Putrajaya first, drop bags at hotel, come back up. +50–60 min. Only if Sentral storage full.
- 10:10 Grab → 10:35 **Batu Caves**. 272 steps slow, Temple Cave, free. Shorts = rent sarong. Monkeys: zip bag, nothing in hands. Weekday mid-morning = thin crowds. ~70 min.
- 11:50 Grab 15 min → **Titiwangsa Lake**: full-skyline Petronas photo, 15–20 min. (This is how "the north" survives — Zoo + Royal Selangor don't fit, see casualties.)
- 12:30 Grab 12 min → **Brickfields**. Banana leaf lunch — Vishal Food & Catering (or Ananda Bahwan backup). Eat with hand, refills free. ~RM15–20.
- 13:50 walk 15 min via **Masjid Negara** grounds → 14:10 **Islamic Arts Museum** (RM20). 2h, aircon recovery block. Bookshop = quality gifts. Old KL Railway Station facade is on the walk.
- 16:20 fork by battery:
  - **(a) tired:** back to Sentral, 17:00 KLIA Transit → Putrajaya 17:20 → hotel ~17:45. Shower, 19:00 dinner.
  - **(b) alive:** Grab 10 min → Merdeka Square + Sultan Abdul Samad 40-min loop → Sentral 17:45 → Transit 18:00 → hotel 18:40, dinner 19:00 (tight, works).
  - **(c) if Thursday dinner is skippable:** stay in KL — Masjid Jamek dusk, River of Life blue lights ~19:45, Chinatown/Kwai Chai Hong lanterns, Madras-Lane-adjacent dinner, KL Tower 20:30 night slot possible. Grab back to Cyberjaya ~22:00 (RM55–70). This is the strongest version of Thursday. Depends on open question #3.
- 22:00 sleep. Non-negotiable.

Rain fallback: swap Merdeka loop for more museum / Sentral (NU Sentral mall attached).
